## 1. ROOM STRUCTURE

*   **Room Type:** Modern minimalist living room / reading lounge featuring a double-height architectural ceiling cutout.
*   **Visible Walls:**
    *   **Left Boundary:** Floor-to-ceiling glass wall completely covered by full-height semi-sheer white drapery.
    *   **Back Wall (Left-Center):** Flush matte white alcove containing a custom built-in floor-to-ceiling bookshelf.
    *   **Back Wall (Right-Center):** Flat matte white painted wall surface.
    *   **Right Wall / Architectural Divider:** Vertical wall partition with rounded vertical end-caps, separating the living room area from a stairwell.
*   **Floor:** Continuous, seam-free or large-format light grey polished concrete / micro-cement finish with soft specular sheen.
*   **Ceiling:** Flat matte white ceiling plane featuring an organic, curved ceiling cutout (atrium opening) above the left area, allowing light down from above.
*   **Doors / Windows / Openings:**
    *   Large exterior window wall (hidden behind sheer curtains on the left).
    *   Ceiling aperture/void above the left-center area.
    *   Staircase passage on the far right behind the divider wall (3 wooden steps visible).
*   **Proportions & Dimensions (Estimated):**
    *   Width (visible front line): ~6.0 – 6.5 m
    *   Depth: ~4.5 – 5.0 m
    *   Ceiling Height (standard zone): ~2.8 – 3.0 m
    *   Ceiling Height (atrium cutout zone): ~5.0+ m (extends upward)
*   **Surface Orientation:**
    *   *Horizontal:* Floor plane, ceiling plane, bookshelf shelves, staircase treads, sofa seats.
    *   *Vertical:* Curtain plane, back wall plane, right partition wall, TV screen plane, bookshelf side walls/back, book spines.
*   **Camera / Viewpoint:** Eye-level to low perspective shot (~1.2m elevation), central-right biased angle looking slightly left-of-center towards the corner and curtains.

## 3. SPATIAL RELATIONSHIPS

1.  **Sofa placement:** Situated directly on the floor in the center-left space, aligned horizontally with the room axis. It rests ~0.5m in front of the sheer drapery wall and its rightmost cushion terminates in front of the left cabinet door of the built-in bookshelf.
2.  **TV & Stand placement:** Positions itself in the right section of the room, standing freestanding ~0.3m away from the back wall, framed between the right edge of the bookshelf unit and the left edge of the rounded stair divider wall.
3.  **Pendant Lamp positioning:** Suspended vertically from above, floating centrally above the green sofa's left-center seating module at approximately eye-level elevation relative to a standing person (~1.8m above floor level).
4.  **Bookshelf Integration:** Recessed neatly flush into the back wall niche, filling the height from the lower cabinet top to the upper ceiling soffit edge.
5.  **Divider Wall & Stairs:** The rounded partition wall forms a sightline barrier on the right side of the room, shielding the wooden staircase behind it from the main living area view.

## 5. MATERIAL ANALYSIS

*   **Sage Green Velvet (Sofa):**
    *   *Base Color:* RGB(0.32, 0.42, 0.25) / Olive-Sage Green.
    *   *Roughness:* ~0.55 (Base), with strong retroreflection/sheen effect on edges.
    *   *Normal Map:* Micro-cloth weave with soft cushion indentation creases.
*   **Polished Concrete / Micro-cement (Floor):**
    *   *Base Color:* Light neutral greige RGB(0.80, 0.78, 0.75).
    *   *Roughness:* Roughness map ranging from 0.25 to 0.45 (glossy spots near light sources).
    *   *Reflectivity:* Subtle Fresnel ground reflections of sofa and light sources.
*   **Matte Off-White Wall/Ceiling Paint:**
    *   *Base Color:* Warm off-white RGB(0.92, 0.91, 0.88).
    *   *Roughness:* High (~0.85 – 0.95).
    *   *Reflectivity:* Almost non-reflective diffuse scattering.
*   **Semi-Translucent Sheer Fabric (Curtains):**
    *   *Base Color:* White RGB(0.95, 0.95, 0.95).
    *   *Transmission/Opacity:* ~40–50% light transmission, alpha mask for sheer effect.
*   **Light Oak Wood (Stair Treads):**
    *   *Base Color:* Light natural warm wood RGB(0.82, 0.70, 0.55).
    *   *Texture:* Straight oak wood grain with moderate gloss varnish (Roughness ~0.35).

## 6. LIGHTING

*   **Primary Light Source (Daylight):** Strong diffuse natural light entering from the left side through the sheer window wall. Creates broad soft shadows casting rightward across the floor plane.
*   **Secondary Light Source (Top Aperture):** Natural skylight illuminating downward from the curved ceiling void directly onto the bookshelf and curtain upper area.
*   **Artificial Lights:**
    *   *Pendant Lamp:* Off or unlit ambient fill (no active point/spot emission visible on floor underneath).
    *   *Floor Lamp:* Off.
*   **Shadow Dynamics:** Soft contact shadows directly under the green sofa modules, TV tripod feet, and bookshelf floor edge. No harsh direct sun shadows; fully diffused overcast/filtered daylight profile.
*   **Overall Ambient Color Temperature:** Cool daylight (~5500K - 6000K) coming from left window, balanced by warm bounce off white walls (~4000K).

## 8. AR REQUIREMENTS & FEASIBILITY

### Realistically Covertible to 3D AR Scene:
1.  **Standalone Furniture Assets:** Green modular sofa, TV on easel stand, floor lamp, and pendant light can easily be isolated, converted into GLB assets, and placed on real-world AR floor planes.
2.  **Color & Scale Fidelity:** Materials like velvet, plastic, wood, and concrete maps can be accurately translated using standard PBR textures (BaseColor, Roughness, Normal, Metallic, Transmission).

### Limitations / Non-Recoverable Single-Image Data:
1.  **Hidden Geometry:** Exact depth of sofa back, rear structure of TV, back of bookshelf, interior of stairs cannot be seen and must be procedurally generated or modeled standardly.
2.  **Environment Boundary:** The spatial bounding box of the entire room (exact wall distance behind camera) is unresolvable; for mobile AR, furniture should be decoupled from static room architecture (walls/ceilings) unless deploying as a fixed spatial scene.

## 10. AR PLACEMENT STRATEGY

*   **Ground Anchor:** The main floor plane (y=0 in 3D space) is the primary tracking surface.
*   **Fixed Relative Group:**
    *   *Set A (Living Set):* Green Modular Sofa + Floor Lamp maintain relative spacing.
    *   *Set B (Media Set):* TV Stand Assembly sits at fixed relative orientation opposite or offset from sofa.
*   **Independent Interactive Objects:**
    *   The user should be able to move/rotate the Modular Sofa or TV Assembly individually in AR onto their real floor.
    *   Architectural elements (Bookshelf, Wall, Stairs, Curtains) can be toggled OFF when placing furniture into a real user room, or toggled ON for virtual room preview/VR mode.

---

## 11. SCALE ESTABLISHMENT

*   **Primary Reference Object for Scale Calibration:** TV Screen / Easel Stand Assembly. Standard TV screen dimensions provide reliable real-world bounds:
    *   Standard 65-inch diagonal display = ~1.43m width.
    *   Standard seat height of modular designer lounges (Bellini Camaleonda) = ~0.40m seat height / ~0.67m overall height.
*   **Manual Confirmation Needed:** Overall wall-to-wall room depth and height of ceiling cutout opening must be set via assumed architectural standards (e.g., floor to ceiling = 3.0m).
